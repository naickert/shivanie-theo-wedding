# 03 — RSVP Reminder

**When to send:** Late October 2026 (~23–26 October, about a week before the 31 October RSVP deadline)
**Audience:** Only parties who have NOT yet RSVP'd
**Tone:** Gentle, friendly, no guilt

---

## Variables

- `{{first_name}}` — guest's first name
- `{{invite_link}}` — personalised link

---

## Message

👋 Hi {{first_name}},

Just a gentle nudge — we haven't received your RSVP yet, and we'd love to know if you'll be able to celebrate with us in December. RSVPs close on *Saturday, 31 October*.

Your personalised invite is here:
{{invite_link}}

It only takes a minute. 💛

If you've already replied, please ignore this — and thank you!

— T & S
